04 — SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

Sized for the machine you already own.

Claim Forge runs its models on your hardware, not on our servers — so the honest question is what your machine can hold in fast memory. Every performance figure on this page is a pre-release estimate; first-party benchmarks land before launch.

03 — SETUP & CONNECTIVITY

Online once. Offline after.

During setup — a connection is required

The Ollama runtime and the model weights (Gemma-class 12B (reasoning) via Ollama) download once over the internet. The initial model download genuinely requires a connection — the weights are gigabytes, and there is no way around that.

Every day after — fully offline

Interviewing, drafting, the encrypted vault, and export all run without a network. Voice extras are optional and download the same one-time way:

faster-whisper — local, offline
