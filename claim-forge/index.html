01 — WORKFLOW

Idea to patent draft, in five gated steps.

A gated pipeline, not a black box. Every stage produces something you can inspect — and approve — before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Describe the invention by voice or typing, and add at least one original visual. No visual, no draft — that rule never bends.

  2. 02

    Understanding

    The local model restates your invention in plain language, then asks focused follow-up questions until the details hold together.

  3. 03

    Approved material

    You correct anything wrong and approve the understanding. Only approved material becomes evidence for drafting.

  4. 04

    Drafting

    Specialized agents draft the disclosure, detailed description, and claim outlines — every limitation linked to its source.

  5. 05

    Attorney-ready export

    Export a flattened PDF review package when you choose, to a folder you choose. The working DOCX stays sealed in the vault.

04 — CAPABILITIES

What it does, concretely.

01

Guided voice + typed intake

Speak or type — voice is the default, typing is first-class. faster-whisper transcribes locally, and every answer is preserved as encrypted evidence.

02

Evidence-backed drafting

Every drafted statement and proposed claim limitation traces to inventor-approved material. Uncertainty and contradictions are shown, never smoothed over.

03

Claim outlines with dependent fallbacks

Independent claim outlines with dependent fallback positions, each limitation linked to its support in the approved record.

04

Independent quality review

The agent that drafts never grades its own work — an independent quality pass checks terminology, reference numbers, and unsupported matter.

05

Encrypted local vault

Each project lives in a Fernet-encrypted vault at ~/.claimforge. The key derives from your passphrase — 480,000 PBKDF2 iterations — and never touches disk.

06

Editable DOCX + flattened-PDF export

The working DOCX stays inside the vault. Export a flattened PDF package, drawings included, to a folder you choose — export is always an explicit act.
