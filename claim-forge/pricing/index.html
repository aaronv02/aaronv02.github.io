Personal is scoped for individual inventors evaluating the workflow and drafting their own applications — non-commercial evaluation. Professional and Studio are sized for commercial drafting work: regular client deliverables, the 24B drafting model, shared team pipelines.

04 — UPDATES & SUPPORT

Signed offline updates

Policy placeholder

Every license includes a fixed window of signed offline updates — one year on Personal, three on Professional, five on Studio. Packages are Ed25519-signed, applied from a local file, and verified offline before anything is installed; the app never phones home to check. When the window ends, the app keeps working — you simply keep the last version you received. An optional renewal will extend the window; renewal pricing will be announced before launch.
