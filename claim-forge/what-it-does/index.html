From a spoken idea to a structured draft.

CAPABILITY LEDGER

What exists, and what is honestly planned.

Every row below is either running in the product today or tagged PLANNED. Nothing planned is presented as shipped.

In the product today

  • INTAKE

    Guided voice + typed intake

    Speak or type your explanation — both are preserved verbatim as encrypted evidence you can correct at any time.

  • GATE

    Required original-visual checkpoint

    The workflow stops until you upload at least one original sketch, photo, diagram, or CAD image — no visual, no formal draft.

  • APPROVAL

    Plain-language understanding, inventor-approved

    The Understanding Agent restates your invention in plain language, and nothing is drafted until you approve that restatement.

  • DIALOG

    Follow-up questions & corrections

    Focused questions close gaps, and corrections or “I do not know yet” are recorded as first-class answers.

  • SECTIONS

    Full application sections

    Disclosure, detailed description, claims with dependent fallback positions, an abstract counted against the 150-word statutory limit, and figure descriptions.

  • TRACE

    Limitation-to-evidence support map

    Every proposed claim limitation links back to the inventor-approved passage or visual it rests on.

  • REVIEW

    Independent quality passes

    A model that did no drafting runs terminology, factual-support, design-around, and attorney-style review — the drafter never grades itself.

  • FLAGS

    Inventorship flags for human review

    Possible co-inventor contributions are quoted from your own words and flagged; the legal determination stays with humans.

  • VAULT

    Encrypted vault + append-only audit

    Each project lives encrypted at ~/.claimforge, and every meaningful action is appended to a tamper-evident log.

  • DOCX

    Application package in the vault

    The editable DOCX package is assembled from approved drafts and stored inside the encrypted vault — it is never released.

  • EXPORT

    Flattened PDF + encrypted .cfpdf

    Release is explicit: a flattened PDF with drawings to a folder you choose, or an encrypted .cfpdf for off-machine transfer.

  • HISTORY

    Versioned drafts

    Draft revisions are versioned, so earlier states of the application stay recoverable.

Planned / conceptual

  • PLANNED

    Prior-art research organization

    Collect and organize public patent documents in a separate, non-confidential environment that never receives the invention.

  • PLANNED

    Deeper contradiction & omission tracking

    Longer-memory tracking of contradictions, omissions, and open questions across sessions and long interviews.

  • PLANNED

    Additional-view requests

    The Visual Agent will ask for specific extra angles when the visuals on file cannot support the description.

  • PLANNED

    Team collaboration

    Shared vaults and multi-person review are deferred until the single-inventor foundation is proven.

Automatic filing, legal conclusions, and cloud storage of confidential material are explicitly out of scope.

BOUNDARIES

What it is not.

Not a law firm

Claim Forge is drafting software. It employs no attorneys and provides no counsel.

Not legal advice

Nothing it produces is a legal opinion, a recommendation, or a filing strategy.

No guarantee of patentability

It never predicts or promises an outcome at any patent office.

No replacement for attorney review

Have a qualified patent attorney or agent review anything you intend to file.
