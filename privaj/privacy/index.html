Privacy model

Privacy isn't a setting. It's the architecture.

Most AI tools work by sending your data to a shared cloud service. Privaj is built the opposite way: the whole system runs on hardware you control, so your customers' information never has to leave the building.

What stays on your hardware

  • The AI model that powers your agents
  • Every conversation with every agent
  • The agent's memory and saved notes
  • The business files and documents you share with it
  • The record of actions each agent has taken

What the cloud touches

To let you reach your agents remotely, a thin relay brokers the connection between your browser and your machine. It is designed to pass your session through — not to store your conversations, files, or credentials. This public website stores none of your business data at all.

The only information the Privaj control plane keeps is the minimum needed to sign you in, know which hardware you own, and show whether it's online. Any cloud storage of sensitive content is an explicit, documented opt-in — never the default.

Owned or managed — same promise

If you own the server, you hold the hardware and can disconnect it whenever you like. If Privaj hosts it for you, your deployment is isolated from every other customer, and the data custodianship terms are spelled out in writing.

Transparency by default

Because the agent runs on your machine, you can see what it can and can't do, and review what it has done on your behalf. There are no hidden actions and no silent data collection.
