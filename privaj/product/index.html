The hardware

Two ways to run it. One privacy model.

Privaj runs on a dedicated machine so your data and AI model stay in one place you trust. Choose who holds that machine.

On your premises

A configured dedicated server lives in your shop or office. Everything — model, conversations, memory, files — runs on it. This is the highest-privacy option: you hold the hardware and can take it offline anytime.

Managed by Privaj

Prefer not to buy or maintain hardware? Privaj runs high-spec machines and hosts your deployment in full isolation from every other customer, with a written data agreement. Same experience, nothing for you to manage.

What's included

  • A configured machine with the AI model pre-installed
  • One or more agents set up for your business workflows
  • Secure remote access through the Privaj relay
  • Setup and onboarding by a technician
  • Ongoing support
