    How it gets made

    The loop is simple. Running it relentlessly is the craft.

    1. 01

      Frame

    2. 02

      Prototype

      Work with AI to get a rough, working version in front of a real user within days, not months.

    3. 03

      Real usage breaks things. Feed every failure back into the loop until it stops breaking.

      Put it in production, watch it run, and move to the next idea with everything learned.
