About me

Aaron Vajda

Builder · problem solver · soon-to-be NAU graduate

I’m Aaron Vajda — a builder who turns everyday problems into working software. I’m about to graduate from Northern Arizona University with a degree in Business Management, hopefully with honors, and I’ve spent that time proving something to myself: that one motivated person with the right tools can ship real products people actually use. Everything on this site — an on-premise AI platform for local businesses, a voice-first patent drafter, an email sorter, a realtor’s analysis bot, and my own local-first dashboard — I designed and built end to end, with AI in the loop the whole way. I’m a hard-working problem solver who’s at my best in the gap between “this is annoying” and “this is handled.” I care about privacy and about tools that respect the people using them, and I don’t leave things half-finished. If you’ve got a workflow that deserves software, I’d love to build it.
